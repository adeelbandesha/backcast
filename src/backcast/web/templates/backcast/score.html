The file

One row per forecast, with a header row. date is the trading day whose close you forecast from, and forecast is the price you expected that many trading days later, scored as the median. 

date,forecast,q10,q90
2025-03-03,241.10,232.50,249.80
2025-03-04,238.75,230.20,247.40

q10 and q90 are optional: with them you also get the 80% interval's coverage and score. Add q20 to q80 as well and CRPS is scored too.

You need at least {{ min_origins }} forecasts whose target date has passed; later ones are left out. The prices have to go back {{ trend_days }} trading days before your first forecast, and Backcast loads five years of them, so start within about the last four years. The file can be up to {{ max_upload|intcomma }} bytes.

Backcast checks the dates but can't tell whether a forecast used prices from after its date. Nothing you upload is saved: the results are on this page only, so there's no link to share.
